WoWs Tactics Suite — third-party notices
==========================================

wows-toolkit
------------

The replay viewer add-on reads replays with the suite's own parser, whose
knowledge of the replay format (the encryption, the packet stream, the entity
definitions and how the old minimap renderer drew a battle) owes a great deal
to landaire's wows-toolkit (https://github.com/landaire/wows-toolkit). None of
its code ships with this app.


Electron, Chromium and Node.js
------------------------------

The app runs on Electron, which carries its own notices in the install folder:
LICENSE.electron.txt and LICENSES.chromium.html.


World of Warships
-----------------

World of Warships is a trademark of Wargaming. This is an unofficial tool, not
made by or endorsed by Wargaming. It reads the game's own files and replays on
your machine. It ships a few of the game's images and data with it: the
minimap images, ship icons and ship data (seed/maps, seed/ships) and the
battle HUD's kill icons (seed/battle_hud). These belong to Wargaming and are
not covered by the app's license.
