wows-toolkit
------------

The replay viewer add-on is built on landaire's wows-toolkit
(https://github.com/landaire/wows-toolkit), used through the crates
wows_replays, wowsunpack, wows_minimap_renderer, wows-battle-world and
wows-replay-insights. Everything difficult about reading a replay is theirs:
decrypting the file, parsing the packet stream, simulating the battle world,
deciding what each side could see, and resolving all of it into draw commands.
This app only takes those commands and draws them on its own map.
